BIM Program Details

What You Learn in This Executive Diploma

A complete BIM curriculum designed for architects, civil engineers, MEP professionals and construction managers — focused on real project workflows and global standards.

Revit Architecture

From concept modelling to documentation and collaboration for residential and commercial buildings.

Revit Structure

Structural modelling, reinforcement, analysis preparation and documentation for concrete and steel structures.

Revit MEP

Design HVAC, plumbing, electrical and fire-fighting systems with coordinated BIM workflows.

Navisworks & BIM 360

Clash detection, 4D/5D simulation and cloud collaboration for real construction projects.
